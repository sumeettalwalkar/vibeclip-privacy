Privacy Policy for VibeClip

Last updated: June 2025

VibeClip ("we", "our", or "us") operates the VibeClip mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our app.

Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, username, profile photo
  • User content: videos, comments, messages you post
  • Camera and microphone: used only when you record video or go live
  • Device information: device type, OS version, unique device identifiers
  • Usage data: screens visited, videos watched, features used
  • Location: general location (country/region) only

How we use your data

  • To provide and improve the VibeClip service
  • To personalise your content feed and recommendations
  • To send notifications about activity on your account
  • To detect and prevent fraud, spam, and abuse
  • To comply with legal obligations

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with cloud infrastructure providers and analytics services (aggregated, anonymised data only).

Children's privacy

VibeClip is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
